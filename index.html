About Me

Economist dedicated to foundational microeconomics, strategic equilibrium, and policy analysis.

Hello, I am Siwat Teimtad. My approach to economics bridges classical first-principles intuition with modern strategic mathematics. I believe that sound economic insight comes from asking two vital questions: What are the underlying incentives? and What are the unseen secondary consequences?

Whether analyzing the subtle welfare distortions of regulatory market interventions or formulating payoff matrices for strategic competition, my work centers on demystifying economic mechanics for decision-makers, students, and practitioners.

02 // Framework

The Economic Mindset

Two cornerstone methodologies that define analytical clarity.

1. Bastiat-Hazlitt Axiom

Good economics requires evaluating both what is immediately visible and what remains unseen. Every policy creates primary outcomes for concentrated groups and secondary unintended costs across dispersed populations.

2. Strategic Nash Optimization

Outcomes in competitive environments depend on mutual expectations. We model incentives to identify whether equilibria lead to collaborative Pareto-superior gains or competitive Prisoner's Dilemma traps.
